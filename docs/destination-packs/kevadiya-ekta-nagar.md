## How to read the rates

Every room has **1 or 2 rate columns**, printed by the supplier under whatever labels
appear below (shown in **bold** before each figure). The two columns almost never mean
the same thing twice — they can be weekday/weekend, single/double occupancy, two meal
plans, or two half-year seasons. **The column meaning is stated per room below — never
assume, always read the label.**

- `On Request` = the supplier printed no number for that cell. Never invented — if it says
  On Request, the real figure must be confirmed with the supplier directly.
- **Extra person/bed supplement** = the charge for an extra guest in that room, where printed
  (per person, typically per night — confirm the exact basis with the supplier before quoting).
- **Extra meal supplement** = a per-meal add-on figure, where printed, sometimes broken out per
  meal plan (e.g. a different figure for CPAI vs MAPAI).
- **Meal plan** is only shown separately when the room's two rate columns are NOT already the
  meal plans themselves (i.e. axis is not 'Two different meal plans') — otherwise the column
  labels already say it and repeating it would look like both columns share one plan.
- Rates are **per room per night** unless a room's own note says otherwise.
- All figures below are the supplier's NET rate (what this agency pays) — no markup applied.
